Problem statment:
Our target users are employers for big companies, who are experiencing a hard time sifting through many resumes, do not want to add their own personal biases, and want to make sure the possible employee they hire is suitable for the job, or if the resume is readable.

User Input:
Hiring requirements for the company.
Resumes for the AI to process.

Use of AI:
AI processes and checks the resume.
AI will output the suitability of the possible employee from an unbiased stance.
Categorize applicants into different tiers, placing applicants from most suitable to least suitable, based off their history for relevancy, soft and hard skills

Business Rules:
Reject all applicants with a criminal record e.g = crypto heists
Reject all applicants that meet the hiring requirements for the company
